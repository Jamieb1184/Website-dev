About the photographer

"When you photograph people in color, you photograph their clothes. But when you photograph people in Black and White, you photograph their souls!" ~ Ted Grant

Jamie discovered her passion for photography at the age of 16, admiring the black and white photos of skateboarders and musicians in magazines. Attending school for Fine Commercial Art, she discovered her love for black and white imagery while gaining the technical knowledge to pursue photography further. Believing that the absence of color allows the imagination to make up the story, she has leaned into black and white work to develop her own unique style.
